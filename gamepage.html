A Mission Full of Suspicion.

Players take on the roles of Crewmates or Impostors aboard a spaceship. Crewmates must complete their assigned tasks and identify the Impostors, while Impostors must secretly eliminate Crewmates and sabotage the mission without being discovered.

The Mission

  • Complete tasks around the spaceship.

  • Report suspicious activity.

  • Discuss and vote during meetings.

  • Identify the Impostors before it is too late.
